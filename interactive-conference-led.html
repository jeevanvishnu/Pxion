Key Features & Benefits

Multi-Touch Interaction

Responsive 20-point infrared touch technology for collaborative whiteboarding and presentations.

Anti-Glare Coating

Specialized surface treatment eliminates reflections from classroom and conference room lighting.

Wireless Screencasting

Seamlessly share content from laptops, tablets, and smartphones without cables.
