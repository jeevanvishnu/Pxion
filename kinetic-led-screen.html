Engineering

Design & Build Features

Precision engineering meets visual innovation in every kinetic unit.

Die-casting Cabinet Design

Built with lightweight, die-cast aluminum cabinets designed for quick installation, dismantling, and portability — ideal for touring events and permanent installations alike.

Seamless Visuals

Engineered with tight gap tolerances between units to maintain visual continuity even as mechanical modules move — delivering a flawless, uninterrupted image at all positions.

Synchronized Motion Control

Each module's stroke is precisely controlled and synchronized with video content via a dedicated control system — enabling complex wave and 3D effect choreography in real time.

Fast Response & High Reliability

Max forward speed of 600mm/s with a response time of 0.4 seconds. Industrial-grade linear guide rails minimize unit gaps and ensure consistent mechanical performance.

Common Applications

Where Kinetic Screens Excel

From high-impact retail to spectacular event stages — kinetic screens command attention everywhere.

Commercial Advertising

High-impact storefronts, shopping malls, and brand activations that demand attention. Turn any commercial space into a living visual statement.

Digital Exhibition Halls & Museums

Interactive, futuristic installations that transform passive visitors into active participants — perfect for science centres, art museums, and cultural institutions.

Events, Stages & Rentals

Dynamic backdrops for concerts, fashion shows, and corporate exhibitions. The motorized movement adds a physical dimension no static display can replicate.

High-End Retail & Brand Showrooms

Luxury flagship stores and premium showrooms use kinetic screens to create unforgettable immersive brand experiences that set them apart from competitors.
