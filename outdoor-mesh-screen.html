Mesh Transparent Screen

Designed for building facades and large-scale digital canvases. Our mesh transparent screens offer exceptional wind resistance, front & rear serviceability, and high daylight brightness without compromising natural light.

Benefits

Key Features & Benefits

Discover what makes Mesh Transparent Screen the ideal choice for your business

High Transparency

Up to 70% see-through rate allows natural light into buildings while displaying vibrant video outside.

Wind Pass-Through

Aerodynamic mesh design dramatically reduces wind-load, making it safe for skyscraper facades.

Lightweight Structure

Fraction of the weight of standard LED walls, requiring minimal architectural reinforcement.

Front & Rear Service

Modular mesh design allows for easy front or rear maintenance, saving time and structural costs.

High Brightness

Ultra-high brightness LEDs ensure outstanding visibility even under direct sunlight.

Weatherproof & Durable

IP65/IP68 rated modules built to withstand harsh outdoor conditions, rain, and storms.

Wind-Resistant Structure

High transparency allows wind to pass through, reducing structural load.

Ultra-Lightweight

Minimal impact on the weight capacity of building facades.
