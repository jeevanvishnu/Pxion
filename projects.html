Workflow

Deployment Process

Delivering top structural calibration standards from engineering sketch to ongoing support loops.

01

Spatial Audit & Needs Assessment

Our engineers perform detailed spatial audits checking viewing ranges, ambient light indices, and wall loads to draft structural blueprints.

02

Design & Hardware Engineering

Selecting custom diode pitches, bezel boundaries, failover backup pathways, and layout controls calibrated for your environment.

03

Structural Mounting & Anchoring

certified professionals execute frame anchors alignment, modular panel locking adjustments, and organized clean cabling frameworks.

04

Pixel Calibration & Balance

Applying millimetric adjustments to brightness levels, gray scales uniformity, and primary gamuts using high-end spectrophotometer equipment.

05

IoT Diagnostics Telemetry Support

Organizing automatic alert telemetry hooks, providing complete staff training guides, and allocating 24/7 priority maintenance plans.
